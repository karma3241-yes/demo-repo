# Roadmap

Planned work that isn't in the game yet.

## Done

- **Multiplayer (PvP)**: room codes on the public site (peer-to-peer), and automatic rooms in the claude.ai version.
- **Re-choosing powers**: **Change powers** in the pause menu, once every 10 minutes, with skill points refunded for dropped powers.
- **Skill point reset (respec)**: **Reset skill points** on the Skills screen.
- **Shared city in room-code games**: NPCs, traffic, crimes, destruction and time of day come from the host.
- **Proper ragdoll physics** for launched and defeated characters.

## Next up

- **Shared city in the claude.ai version.** Room-code games share the host's city; the claude.ai room still runs a city per player.
- **Rooms that survive the host leaving.** Room-code games end when the host closes the game. Handing hosting to another player would keep them going.

## Ideas

- More heist types and crime-boss storylines.
- More Morph Band aliens.
